What is Coding Conf?

Coding Conf started as a small meetup in a lecture room and has grown into a full day event for students, junior devs and people who have been shipping code for years. The focus is practical: you should leave with something you can use in your own projects the next morning.

Registration is free. Fill in the form, upload a profile photo and you will get a personal digital ticket with your own seat number. Bring it on the day (on your phone is fine) and show it at the front desk.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Tickets are free, but you need to register so we can plan seating and food.

Why do you need my GitHub username?

It goes on your ticket and on the attendee wall so people can find your work after the event.

What kind of photo should I upload?

A clear JPG or PNG of your face, under 500KB. It is printed on your ticket.

I lost my ticket. What now?

Register again with the same email and we will match it at the desk.

Ready to join us?
